# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

VTC teaching staff attending CLT Training-and-Workshop sessions (SHAPE programme). They receive a bare link, watch the 3:25 video, and complete the quiz on whatever device is in front of them — workshop projector or personal phone. The facilitator (Tylor Lun) is the other audience: distributes the link and edits quiz content.

## Product Purpose

Turn the official VTC "Technology Enhanced Learning" video into an active check of understanding: participants identify the five TEL types according to the M2 course framework. Success = a participant can distinguish framework categories from the technologies the video demonstrates.

## Positioning

A zero-dependency, no-login assessment that deliberately tests the course framework rather than the video's surface content — something a plain video embed cannot offer.

## Operating Context

Shared as a bare GitHub Pages URL in workshop slides/handouts; used on projectors and personal devices; content lives in one config block a facilitator can edit; the video requires YouTube reachability.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Pick-5-of-9 click-to-select with immediate right/wrong feedback on each selection; no submission step — the activity completes when all five framework categories are found, and a Start-again button reloads with a fresh shuffle; options shuffled per load; English only; no accounts, analytics, or persistence; hosted on GitHub Pages. Correct answers = the M2 framework categories (Applied and Digital Skills, Computer-aided Assessment, Smart Learning Facilities, Wearable Technology, Learning Management Platform); distractors VR/AR/MR/Blended Learning. "Blended learning" was deliberately excluded until 14 Sep 2026, when the facilitator requested it as a fourth distractor; the on-screen instruction line was removed the same day. Certificate section removed 10 Sep 2026; submission replaced by instant reveal + start-again on 14 Sep 2026 — both at facilitator request.

## Brand Commitments

VTC CLT / SHAPE Module M2 naming. Professional academic UI in a light orange tone — user-pinned.

## Evidence on Hand

Official VTC video (KBlohpOEJOs, 3:25, English, embeddable); spec = issue #1; engine tests. No official VTC page publishes the five-type list; no photo assets — do not fabricate either.

## Product Principles

1. Framework over surface content.
2. Zero-friction access — bare link, instant load.
3. Honest first attempt.
4. Facilitator-editable content.
5. Zero dependencies, no build step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard and screen-reader operable answer options; visible focus; reduced motion respected. English-only by decision.
